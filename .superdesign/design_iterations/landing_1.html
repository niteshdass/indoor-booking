Questions

Do I need an account to book?

No. Book with your name and phone number. Creating an account lets you see all your bookings and set up weekly games.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, up to the cutoff the venue sets. Refunds go back to the card you paid with.

How much does it cost venues?

Listing is free. Venues pay a small fee per online booking and keep their own Stripe payouts.

Is it available in Russian?

Yes. Switch language from the top of any page, for players and for staff.
