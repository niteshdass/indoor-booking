# Slot Booking — Design System

Adapted 2026-10-24 from the Fluent Monetize design system; visual identity v2 ("Hard court") proposed 2026-10-26.
Product context and booking components are this project's.
Source of truth in code: `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css`. Every UI change uses these tokens — no hardcoded hex, px font sizes, radii or shadows outside that file.

## Product context
Multi-vendor booking for indoor and outdoor sports venues (courts, pitches, lanes). Owners run one or more venues with staff; players find a venue, pick a slot and pay with Stripe. English and Russian.

Surfaces:
- **Owner portal** (React SPA): left sidebar nav + top bar; owner switcher (a user can belong to several owners); dashboard with booking/revenue stat cards; schedule/calendar per venue and court; data tables (bookings, members, staff, activity log); editors (venue, court, pricing, roles); settings forms.
- **Public venue page**: venue header (name, rating, address, sports, Share), photo gallery, court tabs, date strip, slot grid grouped by time of day, booking summary with price and Book button, amenities and location map, reviews.
- **Player**: sign-in (phone OTP, email, Google), checkout, booking receipt, my bookings, account.

JTBD: owners and staff see today's schedule and act on it fast and calmly; players trust the page enough to book and pay.

## Visual identity v2 — "Hard court" (adopted 2026-10-26; graphite look 2026-10-31)

**Look update, 2026-10-31.** Picked by the owner from the look preview:
- Font: IBM Plex Sans, with IBM Plex Sans Condensed for headings.
- Main color: graphite + ball. Graphite in light mode, ball yellow in dark mode.
- Corners: medium, unchanged.
- Spacing: roomy.
- Cards: flat.

Where this section and the sections below disagree, the token tables, Typography and Spacing below win. Older "court blue", "Onest/Unbounded" and "shadow-xs card" wording in component notes now means graphite, Plex and the flat tint.
Mockups: `.superdesign/design_iterations/landing_1.html`, `admin_today_1.html`. The tokens live in `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css`; this section is the rule.

Idea: the look of a sports court. Hard-court blue, chalk lines, a little ball-yellow. The court-line drawing is the one bold device. Everything else stays calm.

### Principles
- **Court lines are the signature.** 2px chalk or primary strokes, top-down court drawings (padel, futsal, tennis, badminton, basketball, volleyball) as the illustration system. No stock photos or gradient blobs.
- **Spend boldness once per screen.** Landing: the hero, a `--hero-bg` tinted band where the padel panel sits on a fan of clay and parquet courts (one screen, every sport). Portal: the live court timeline. Everything around it is quiet: neutral surfaces, soft shadows, status color only where status lives.
- **Containers have no border and no resting shadow (flat).** Cards, panels, table wrappers, stat cards and list items are a `--bg-surface` tint on the lighter `--bg-page`; `--shadow-xs`/`sm` are `none` in both themes. Floating layers (dialogs, menus, dropdowns, pickers, sheets) use `--bg-surface-raised` with `--shadow-md`/`lg`. A box nested inside a card is a `--bg-subtle` inset, not an outlined box. Hover and active states lift (`--shadow-sm`/`md` plus `--bg-surface-raised`) instead of changing a border colour. Borders stay only where they mean something: selection controls (court tabs, choice chips, slot cells, map pill), a focused input, hairline dividers inside a container (`--border-subtle`), and status bars.
- **Ball yellow is the dark-mode primary.** In dark mode it marks primary buttons, active tab and nav, links and focus. In light mode it stays scarce: only the CTA on night surfaces and the hero ball. Never on light backgrounds.
- **Owner brand color** (`--brand`) still only on public venue pages (CTA, active tab, selected slot). Never in portal chrome or on the marketing site.
- **Motion lives on the landing, tied to the court.** Hero: court lines draw in and the slot card rises, then the card loops the booking story in four numbered steps (Find: a mini search bar types "Padel / Today" and a court turns up; Pick: 19:00 gets tapped; Book: summary and the Book button press; Done: the confirmation tick draws), about 10s a lap and only while on screen. The ball keeps a slow cross-court rally. These are the only two loops. Sport tiles re-chalk their court lines on hover or focus. The owners timeline fills its bookings in sequence when it scrolls into view. No generic fade-up on sections, nothing in the portal. `prefers-reduced-motion` shows end states (the card holds on Done) and a still ball.
- **Russian fits.** Strings run 20–30% longer: no fixed-width buttons, tabs or nav items; headlines use `text-wrap: balance`; KPI numbers `nowrap` and shrink with `clamp`.
- Avoid: all-caps eyebrow labels, "→" in buttons, numbered markers on things that are not a sequence (the "how it works" steps are a real sequence, so they are numbered), identical card grids for every section.

### Typography
- **IBM Plex Sans Condensed** (500/600/700, `--font-display`):
  - Used for display and section headings on the landing, page titles (h1) and KPI numbers in the portal.
  - Letter-spacing -0.01em.
  - Never body text, never below 18px.
- **IBM Plex Sans** (400/500/600/700, `--font-sans`):
  - Used for all UI and body text.
  - Prices, times, tables and schedules use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

### Search page (`/search`)
Title "Find a court" in the display font. The search bar is the landing hero pill, kept compact (Sport, Date at `--text-base` with `--space-1`/`--space-3` segment padding, a `md` brand Search button; stacks below sm). Under it a row of `control-md` pill chips: location (turns `--primary-soft` with a `control-sm` clear button once set), Distance and Sort as labelled pill selects, and "More filters" with a count of active filters. Without a location the Distance chip is dimmed and its "Use your location…" hint is a tooltip and screen-reader description, not a visible line. More filters toggles an inline surface panel (padding and gap `--space-4`), never a modal: one flat grid of `md` fields (indoor · from · to · currency · min · max, auto-fill at `--field-min`, so 3×2 in the list column; phones pair From|To and Min|Max with the selects across the row), then the "Only venues with free slots" checkbox (`control-md`, `--text-sm`). The time-range rule and the "Price per slot" legend are for screen readers only. Above the list, beside the venue count, a Rows/Grid icon toggle (from sm, `aria-pressed`, remembered in `localStorage` `search.layout`, default Rows) sits before the List/Map toggle. Rows: horizontal cards from sm, media column `--result-media` 13rem (min height `--result-min` 10rem).
